# Long campaign reports

These reports follow autonomous investigations through their original wall-time
budgets. They record the scientific question, numerical coverage, model usage,
compute accounting, failures and unresolved results. Small public data bundles
support the reported arithmetic without exposing deployment details.

The [48 hour FRC campaign](frc-48-hour-campaign-20261014.md) used eight RTX 2080 Ti
GPUs and separate worker, director and review contexts. It ended with the merger
hypothesis unresolved. The report includes retained plasma fields, numerical
controls, a late polling failure, API pricing scenarios and GPU rental sensitivity.
